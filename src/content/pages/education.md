## Rochester Institute of Technology

*Master's in Computer Science, 2018 to 2021*

I moved to the US for this, looking for deeper technical understanding and challenges. Here's what I focused on:

- **Distributed systems:** I took the course and did an independent study because I wanted to understand everything about what goes on in the background: all the components, consensus algorithms, and how these systems connect together.
- **Natural language processing (NLP):** Obviously, I wanted to learn machine learning and how it is applied to languages. I think this opened my eyes to NLP concepts, machine learning, and newly introduced concepts like transformers.
- **Networking:** One of the most fascinating courses I took. I learned everything about networking from the bottom up, covering raw physical mediums like Wi-Fi, Ethernet, and mobile towers, bits and bytes, errors and error correction, the 7 layers of the OSI model, TCP/IP, congestion control, routing algorithms, ISPs, and many more. *(I built two protocols from scratch: one like a reliable TCP, and the other Routing Information Protocol (RIP v2).)*
- **Parallel computing:** I was curious to know how to process in parallel, with Message Passing Interface (MPI), matrix multiplication, sorting algorithms, broadcast, reduce, and others like NVIDIA's CUDA.
- **Blockchain:** It was a buzzword, so I just wanted to understand what it's all about, how an untrusted system works, and if I could use such concepts somewhere else, which I did in my capstone project based on IOTA.
