## Rochester Institute of Technology

*Master's in Computer Science, 2018 to 2021*

I moved to the US for this, looking for deeper technical challenges. Here's what I focused on, and why:

- Distributed systems: I took the course and did an independent study because I wanted to understand everything about it: how systems connect, what goes behind them, and all those small components connecting together.
- Parallel computing: I wanted to learn how to process in parallel, with Message Passing Interface (MPI), a couple of sort algorithms, broadcast, reduce, and something similar I forgot.
- Natural language processing (NLP): I wanted to learn machine learning and how it is applied to languages, and that got me curious about it.
- Networking: One of the most fascinating courses I took. I learned everything about networking and built two protocols from scratch, one like TCP/IP and the other Routing Information Protocol (RIP) v2.
- Blockchain: Obviously, it was a buzzword, so I just wanted to understand what it's all about, how an untrusted system works, and if I could use it somewhere else, so I used it in my capstone project, IOTA.
