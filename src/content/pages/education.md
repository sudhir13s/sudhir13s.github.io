## Rochester Institute of Technology

*Master's in Computer Science, 2018 to 2021*

I moved to the US for this, looking for deeper technical challenges. Here's what I focused on, and why:

- Distributed systems: I took the course and also did an independent study, because I wanted to understand everything about it: how systems connect, what goes on behind them, cloud computing, and all those small components connecting together.
- Parallel computing: I wanted to learn how to process things in parallel. There was a library called Message Passing Interface (MPI), a couple of sorting algorithms, and operations like broadcast and reduce, and some others I forgot. I think companies like NVIDIA use this or something similar today for parallel computing across many cores.
- Natural language processing (NLP): I took this because I wanted to learn about machine learning and, at the same time, how it is applied to languages. That got me curious about it.
- Networking: This was one of the most fascinating courses I took. I learned everything about networking, and I built two protocols from scratch: one like TCP/IP, and Routing Information Protocol (RIP) version 2.
- Blockchain: Obviously, it was a buzzword. So I just wanted to understand what it's all about: the technologies behind it, the mechanisms that make an untrusted system work, and whether I could use this technology somewhere else. So I used it in my capstone project, on IOTA.
