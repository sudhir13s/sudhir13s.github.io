## Rochester Institute of Technology

*Master's in Computer Science, 2018 to 2021*

I moved to the US for this, looking for deeper technical challenges. Here's what I focused on, and why:

- Distributed systems: I had been building services for years, and I wanted to know what really happens underneath when many machines work together.
- Parallel computing: I took this to get some exposure to running work on many processors at once. It's harder than it sounds.
- Natural language processing (NLP): I was curious how machines make sense of language. My term project was telling very similar languages apart, and I came back to NLP later, working on captions for hard-of-hearing classrooms.
- Blockchain: I wanted to understand it beyond the buzz. My capstone looked at the IOTA Tangle, an alternative to blockchain, for the Internet of Things (IoT).
