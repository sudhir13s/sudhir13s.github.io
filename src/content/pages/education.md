## Rochester Institute of Technology

*Master's in Computer Science, 2018 to 2021*

I moved to the US for this, looking for deeper technical challenges. Here's what I focused on, and why:

- Distributed systems: a course and an independent study, to really understand how systems connect, from cloud computing down to the small components.
- Parallel computing: learned to process across many cores, with Message Passing Interface (MPI), sorting algorithms, and operations like broadcast and reduce.
- Natural language processing (NLP): I wanted to learn machine learning and see how it applies to language, and it got me curious.
- Networking: one of the most fascinating courses I took. I built two protocols from scratch, one like TCP/IP and the other Routing Information Protocol (RIP) v2.
- Blockchain: it was the buzzword, so I wanted to see how an untrusted system actually works, then tried the idea in my capstone on IOTA.
