## Building now

### Interview Prep Hub

*Jun 2026 – Present*

One place to learn system design, AI/ML, software engineering technologies, and domains, with problem solving and ML practice, using a large language model (LLM) as a judge to give feedback.

I'm also trying to add a mock interview module (maybe interview screening for recruiters in the future) and a voice-based interview module, using text-to-speech (TTS), speech-to-text (STT), and an LLM.

This feels like a good product to experiment with. Let's see where it takes me.

### TheAIAtlas

### [miniAgent](https://github.com/sudhir13s/miniAgent)

*Jul 2026*

A mini software development kit (SDK) for building multi-agent AI pipelines. You can swap model providers without changing your code, so you're not locked in to one.
