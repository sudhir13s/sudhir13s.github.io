## Walmart

*2023 to 2025, Sunnyvale*

I worked on the system that decides, for every item on walmart.com, whether it can be sold, shipped, or picked up right now. I built parts of it, ran them in production, and helped a few newer engineers along the way.

## Strateos

*2021 to 2023, Menlo Park*

A cloud lab startup. Scientists submitted experiments from a browser and robots ran them. I worked on turning what a scientist wanted into commands for the robots. I also helped build a colony-picking system with a robotic arm and computer vision. When the startup folded, I moved to Walmart.

## RIT

*2019 to 2021, Rochester*

While doing my Master's, I looked after the CS department's labs and servers, made a clinical trial app load faster during a co-op at Advarra, and worked on better live captions for hard-of-hearing classrooms.

## Philips

*2015 to 2018, Bengaluru*

I built APIs that let patient data move securely between healthcare systems, and for a while looked after a team of 8 developers.

## CliniSys

*2011 to 2015, Bengaluru*

My first job. I learned healthcare from the ground up on lab and pathology systems, and got to own a product end to end, including working directly with clients.
